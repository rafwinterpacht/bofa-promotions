Debit is a fast, easy and secure way to pay in‑store and online

Lock or unlock1 your debit

Misplaced your debit card? You can temporarily lock your debit card, right from our Mobile Banking app or through Online Banking. Then, when you find it, unlocking your card is just as easy.

$0 Liability Guarantee2

Shop with confidence. Our $0 Liability Guarantee means that you’re not liable for unauthorized transactions made using your consumer debit card, as long as you report them promptly.

24/7 monitoring

Fraud monitoring reviews how and where your card is being used and is designed to help block potential fraud if abnormal patterns are detected.

Activate, set up and spend with your debit card

Activate and Use

Activate your card using the Mobile Banking app3 or call 888-624-2323 and start using your card for everyday purchases.

Monitor4

Set up and customize alerts to monitor card activity, balances and payment due dates.

Pay

Need to pay utility, subscription and other bills on a schedule? Add your debit card to your billing account to make recurring payments.

Ways to PAY with your debit card

Enhanced Digital Card for Debit5

Digital card for debit is a digital version of your physical debit card in our Mobile Banking app. It provides industry-leading fraud protection for online purchases. Please note, the digital card for debit can only be added from a mobile device.

Benefits include:

  • Stronger security — Creates a dynamic security code that changes every time it’s viewed.
  • Greater flexibility — Continue to use your digital card for debit even if you lock your physical debit card.
  • Convenience — Access your digital card right from our Mobile Banking app when shopping online.
